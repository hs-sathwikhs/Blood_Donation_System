The fresh demo has two organisers, five donors, five hospitals, six camps,
four registrations, eight requests and stock for all eight blood groups.
Camp and stock dates are relative to the first start. Data persists between runs.
To reset, stop the app and delete data/demoDb.mv.db before starting again.

Try a donor login, reserve and cancel a place in a scheduled camp, then record
one unit at the active camp today. Sign in as a hospital and request blood.
Use the admin account to see the queue, choose a stock allocation order and
fulfill a request. The organiser account can create a draft, publish it and
start it on the scheduled date. Completing a camp closes donation recording.
